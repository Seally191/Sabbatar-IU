Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19/02/2026

This website uses cookies and third-party services. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking here.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when visiting a website.

2. What cookies do we use?

  • Necessary cookies (for basic functionality)
  • Third-party cookies (YouTube videos)

3. YouTube

Our website embeds videos from YouTube. These are only loaded after you give consent. YouTube may set cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policy.

4. Consent

When you first visit the website, you can accept or reject cookies. Your choice is stored in your browser.

5. Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change your preferences at any time by opening cookie settings.

6. Storage

We store your choice in your browser (localStorage). We do not use tracking cookies ourselves.
