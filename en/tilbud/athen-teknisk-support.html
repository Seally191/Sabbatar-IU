Technical Support Specialist in Athens

The Role

Join an international team as a Technical Support Specialist in Athens, Greece. In this role, you will assist customers with technical questions and everyday issues—from setting up devices to troubleshooting common technology problems. It’s a customer service role with a technical twist, where you make technology easier to understand for everyone.



Responsibilities

✓ Assist customers with technical questions via phone, chat, or email.
✓ Guide users through basic setup and troubleshooting steps.
✓ Explain technical solutions in a clear and user-friendly way.
✓ Record and document cases in internal systems.
✓ Collaborate with colleagues and internal teams on more complex issues.
✓ Learn new systems and tools through ongoing training.

The Company

Our partner is a leading provider of customer service solutions, with modern call centers around the world. They specialize in delivering outstanding customer experiences through innovative solutions and a supportive work environment. The Athens office offers a multicultural workplace where employees can grow both professionally and personally, surrounded by a vibrant city and beautiful Mediterranean scenery.

Requirements

➤ You speak fluent Danish or Norwegian and are ready to relocate abroad for at least 6 months.
➤ You are able to organize your relocation with proper financial and personal preparation.
➤ You are eager for new experiences, curious about different cultures, and have a positive mindset.
➤ You are at least 18 years old.
➤ You are an EU, EEA or Nordic citizen with valid documents.

Benefits

✔ Two weeks of paid training before starting.
✔ 14 competitive Greek salaries per year.
✔ Performance-based bonus.
✔ Two weeks of free hotel accommodation upon arrival.
✔ Real estate agency fee covered.
✔ Flight included.
✔ Health insurance, administrative support, and assistance with opening a local bank account.

Location

Experience Athens, Greece – a city where ancient history meets modern living. Enjoy sunny weather, beautiful coastlines, and a rich culture filled with lively markets, incredible food, and iconic landmarks like the Acropolis. Athens offers the perfect balance between work and leisure, with a welcoming local community and endless opportunities to explore.
