Customer Service Representative in Crete

The Role

Join a dynamic team as a Customer Service Representative in Crete, Greece. This role offers an exciting opportunity to work in an international call center, assisting customers from diverse backgrounds while enjoying the Mediterranean culture and sunny climate.



Responsibilities

✓ Respond to customer inquiries in a professional manner.
✓ Provide quick and effective solutions to ensure high customer satisfaction.
✓ Record and document customer interactions and transactions.
✓ Collaborate with colleagues to improve service quality and efficiency.
✓ Participate in regular training to stay updated on products and services.

The Company

Our partner is a leading provider of customer service solutions, with modern call centers around the world. They specialize in delivering outstanding customer experiences through innovative solutions and a supportive work environment. The office in Crete offers a multicultural workplace where employees can grow both professionally and personally, surrounded by a lively atmosphere and beautiful Mediterranean landscapes.

Requirements

➤ You speak fluent Danish or Norwegian and are ready to relocate abroad for at least 6 months.
➤ You are able to organize your relocation with proper financial and personal preparation.
➤ You are eager for new experiences, curious about different cultures, and have a positive mindset.
➤ You are at least 18 years old.
➤ You are an EU, EEA or Nordic citizen with valid documents.

Benefits

✔ Two weeks of paid training before starting.
✔ 14 competitive Greek salaries per year.
✔ Performance-based bonus.
✔ Free accommodation provided throughout your employment.
✔ Flight included.
✔ Health insurance, administrative support, and assistance with opening a local bank account.

Location

Experience Crete – Greece’s largest and most beautiful island. Enjoy sunshine all year round, stunning beaches, crystal-clear waters, and a rich culture with welcoming locals. From scenic mountains and olive groves to charming villages and delicious Greek cuisine, Crete offers the perfect balance between work and a relaxed island lifestyle filled with nature and history.
