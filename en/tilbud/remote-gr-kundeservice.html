Customer Service Representative in Greece

The Role

Join a dynamic team as a Customer Service Representative based in Greece. This role offers an exciting opportunity to work remotely as part of an international customer service team, supporting customers from diverse backgrounds while enjoying the Mediterranean lifestyle and sunny climate.



Responsibilities

✓ Handle customer inquiries in a professional manner.
✓ Provide fast and effective solutions to ensure high customer satisfaction.
✓ Record and document customer interactions and transactions.
✓ Collaborate with colleagues to improve service quality and efficiency.
✓ Participate in regular training to stay up to date on products and services.

The Company

Our partner is a leading provider of customer service solutions, with modern call centers around the world. They specialize in delivering outstanding customer experiences through innovative solutions and a supportive work environment. You will be part of an international team with colleagues from across the globe, offering opportunities for both professional and personal growth in a vibrant and inspiring setting.

Requirements

➤ You speak fluent English and are ready to relocate abroad for at least 6 months.
➤ You are able to organize your relocation with proper financial and personal preparation.
➤ You are eager for new experiences, curious about different cultures, and have a positive mindset.
➤ You are at least 18 years old.
➤ You are an EU citizen with valid documents.

Benefits

✔ Two weeks of paid training before starting.
✔ 14 competitive Greek salaries per year.
✔ Performance-based bonus.
✔ Two weeks of free hotel accommodation upon arrival.
✔ Real estate agency fee covered.
✔ Flight included.
✔ Health insurance, administrative support, and assistance with opening a local bank account.

Location

Experience Greece – a beautiful country full of sunshine, history, and vibrant culture. Enjoy warm weather year-round, stunning beaches, crystal-clear waters, mountains, and islands, along with welcoming locals. From bustling Athens and charming Thessaloniki to idyllic islands and traditional villages, Greece offers the perfect balance between remote work and a relaxed lifestyle filled with great food, culture, and nature.
