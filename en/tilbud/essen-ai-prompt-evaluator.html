AI Prompt Evaluator in Essen

The Role

Join an innovative team as an AI Prompt Evaluator in Essen, Germany. In this role, you will work with analyzing and improving AI-generated content by evaluating prompts and responses. This is an exciting opportunity to work with cutting-edge technology in an international environment while developing your analytical and language skills.



Responsibilities

✓ Evaluate and improve AI-generated prompts and responses.
✓ Ensure quality, relevance, and accuracy of content.
✓ Identify errors, bias, or inconsistencies in data.
✓ Follow guidelines and quality standards.
✓ Collaborate with teams to optimize AI models.

The Company

Our partner is a leading provider of digital solutions and AI services with offices around the world. They specialize in developing and improving advanced technologies through data analysis and innovation. The Essen office offers a modern and international workplace where employees can grow professionally in a rapidly evolving field.

Requirements

➤ You speak fluent Danish and have strong analytical skills.
➤ You are detail-oriented and comfortable working with data.
➤ You are curious about technology and artificial intelligence.
➤ You are at least 18 years old.
➤ You are an EU, EEA or Nordic citizen with valid documents.

Benefits

✔ Two weeks of paid training before starting.
✔ Competitive salary package + bonus.
✔ Modern workplace in an international environment.
✔ Opportunity to work with cutting-edge AI technology.
✔ Career growth opportunities within tech and data.
✔ Stable contract and good working conditions.
✔ Health insurance and administrative support.

Location

Experience Essen, Germany – a city where industrial heritage meets modern innovation. Known for its green spaces, cultural attractions, and central location in the Ruhr region, Essen offers a high quality of life, excellent transport connections, and an international environment—perfect for both work and leisure.
